Why Focus

Built for any screen

One layout that reflows from a single phone column up to a three-pane desktop view.

No clutter

Three colors, two typefaces, one job per screen. Nothing fights for your attention.

Keyboard friendly

Every control is reachable and visibly focusable without touching a mouse.

Why a plain board works better

Most task apps grow features until the list itself gets buried. Focus stays deliberately small: a single board, a simple add action, and a checkbox. That's usually all a day actually needs.
